Privacy Policy

Flex-Auto values your privacy and is dedicated to safeguarding your personal information. This policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection when you interact with our platform.

1. Data Collection

Flex-Auto only gathers personal information when users voluntarily provide it. Any submitted data will be processed solely to deliver our services and enhance your experience on our platform.

2. Cookie Usage

Our website employs minimal cookies, strictly limited to essential operational functions. These may include session management and preference storage. We do not utilize tracking cookies or any technologies that monitor user behavior beyond basic functionality.

3. Third-Party Disclosure

Flex-Auto maintains a strict policy against sharing user data with external parties, except when legally mandated. Our platform operates without third-party advertising networks or analytics trackers.

4. Protection Measures

We employ industry-standard security protocols to protect your information. Please be aware that while we implement robust safeguards, absolute security cannot be guaranteed for any online data transmission.

5. Contact Information

For any inquiries regarding our privacy practices or your personal data, please reach out to our team at contact@flexauto.com.
